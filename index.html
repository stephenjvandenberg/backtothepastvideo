Our Story

Preserving your cherished memories with personal care

We started converting tapes in early 2022, after hearing countless stories from families wanting to convert their taped memories to digital, but dreading the thought of shipping irreplaceable tapes of loved ones across the country in the mail.

We decided to start a dedicated in-person pick-up and delivery service right here in Victorville, where you can meet us personally and have complete confidence that your memories are treated with the highest level of care.

1

Full Beginning-to-End Review

We watch the entire tape from start to finish to ensure not a single second is missed. Accidental recordings of TV shows or movies are never billed to you.

2

Organized & Labeled by Event

No endless fast-forwarding. We cut and splice your files into labeled events so you can jump straight to birthdays, weddings, holidays, and vacations.

3

1-Year Backup Guarantee

Your digitized files are stored locally for at least 1 full year. If you ever misplace your drive or want copies for family members, we have you covered.
